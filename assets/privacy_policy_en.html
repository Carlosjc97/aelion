1. Information we collect

Personal information

  • Email address, display name, and profile photo via Firebase Authentication.
  • Firebase UID to uniquely identify your account.
  • Locale and basic device data via Firebase Analytics.

Usage data

  • Course progress, quiz scores, lesson timestamps, and unlocked modules.
  • Learning goals, onboarding preferences, and diagnostic assessment results.

Analytics and diagnostics

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics for app stability and aggregated events.
  • PostHog to understand feature engagement, session duration, and funnels.

Subscription data

  • Google Play purchase tokens, auto-renewal status, price tier (USD 9.99/month).
  • Expiration dates and history as reported by Google Play Billing.

AI content inputs

  • Topics, objectives, and prompts submitted to personalize your plan.
  • Generated lessons, quizzes, and feedback produced via OpenAI GPT-4.

2. How we use your information

  • Deliver personalized learning paths and AI-generated lessons tailored to your level.
  • Track progress, achievements, and pacing to provide meaningful insights.
  • Authenticate accounts, synchronize data across devices, and restore purchases.
  • Improve our services through aggregated analytics and error diagnostics.
  • Comply with legal obligations, billing requirements, and anti-fraud controls.

3. Data sharing & third parties

We limit sharing to trusted processors bound by data protection agreements:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, hosting, notifications, and Firestore database.
  • OpenAI GPT-4: prompts are transmitted to generate educational content. OpenAI does not use API data to train public models per their policy.
  • PostHog: privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregated usage.
  • Google Play: manages subscriptions, payments, and receipts.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose information only when required by law or to enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Storage & security

  • Firestore security rules enforce authentication and access control.
  • Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS; tokens are verified server-side.
  • We retain data while your account is active or as needed for legal reasons.

5. Your rights

  • Request access or export of your data by contacting privacy@edaptia.io.
  • Update or correct personal information from Settings.
  • Delete your account and personal data from Settings > Privacy or via email.
  • Opt out of optional analytics and notifications within the app.

6. Children's privacy

Edaptia is intended for users aged 13+ (16+ for EU residents). We do not knowingly collect data from children below these thresholds. Parents may contact us to remove unauthorized accounts.

7. AI-generated content

  • Edaptia uses OpenAI GPT-4 to generate lessons, quizzes, and recommendations.
  • AI output may contain inaccuracies or bias. Please verify critical information.
  • Prompts are stored securely and used exclusively to personalize your experience.

8. Changes to this policy

We will notify you via email, in-app notice, or banner before material changes take effect. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.

9. Contact

  • Email: privacy@edaptia.io
  • Company: Aelion Learning
  • Response time: within 30 days.

10. Legal compliance

  • GDPR: We process data under legitimate interest, consent, or contract. EU users may exercise access, portability, correction, or deletion rights.
  • CCPA: California residents may request disclosure or erasure of personal information. We do not sell personal data.
  • Google Play: This policy satisfies Google Play's User Data requirements.
  • Jurisdiction: Governed by the laws of Mexico, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.
